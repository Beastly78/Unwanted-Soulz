**Section 1: Key Features to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**

Before we dive into our top recommendations, let's discuss the key features to look for in a motorcycle helmet. Here are some essential factors to consider:

* **Snell Certification**: Look for a helmet that meets the Snell Memorial Foundation's safety standards, which means it has been tested for impact protection and will provide you with the highest level of safety.
* **Dirt and Water Resistance**: A water-resistant and dirt-repelling helmet is essential for riders who plan to hit the streets or trails. This feature will help keep you dry and comfortable during your rides.
* **Breathability**: A breathable helmet will help keep you cool and prevent sweat buildup, which can lead to discomfort and distraction while riding.
* **Weight**: A light helmet will reduce the fatigue you experience during long rides, making it easier to stay alert and focused.
