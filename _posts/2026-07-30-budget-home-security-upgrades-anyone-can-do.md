# Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do

**Protect Your Home Without Breaking the Bank**

As a homeowner, you want to feel safe and secure in your own home. However, the reality is that many of us can't afford to install a state-of-the-art home security system. But don't worry, there are many budget-friendly home security upgrades you can do on your own to enhance the safety of your home.

In this article, we'll explore 5 simple and affordable ways to boost your home's security without breaking the bank. From DIY home security projects to smart locks and security cameras, we'll show you how to protect your home and family without overspending.

## **Section 1: DIY Home Security Projects**

When it comes to home security, it's the little things that count. Here are a few DIY projects that can make a big difference:

### **Brighten Up Your Home with Motion-Sensing Lights**

Motion-sensing lights are a great way to deter intruders and improve the security of your home. These lights can be installed outside or inside your home and can be set to turn on when motion is detected.

* **Cost:** $20-$50 per light
* **Pros:**
	+ Affordable and easy to install
	+ Energy-efficient and long-lasting
	+ Can be customized to fit your home's exterior
* **Cons:**
	+ May be triggered by pets or kids
	+ Limited to a specific area of your home

### **Secure Your Windows and Doors with Safety Film**

Safety film can be applied to your windows and doors to provide an additional layer of protection against forced entry. This film can be easily applied and removed, making it a great DIY project for homeowners.

* **Cost:** $20-$50 per window or door
* **Pros:**
	+ Affordable and easy to apply
	+ Can be customized to fit your windows and doors
	+ Can help prevent glass breakage during a break-in
* **Cons:**
	+ May not be suitable for all types of windows and doors
	+ Can be difficult to remove if needed

## **Section 2: Smart Locks and Door Security**

Smart locks and door security systems can provide an additional layer of protection for your home. Here are a few options to consider:

* **Cons:**
	+ Requires a smartphone app to control

### **Door Security: Kwikset SmartCode Electronic Lock**

* **Cost:** $99-$149

* **Cons:**
	+ May not be suitable for all types of doors

## **Section 5: Additional Tips and Best Practices**

Here are a few additional tips and best practices to keep in mind when it comes to home security:

### **Tips:**

* Always keep your home well-maintained and free of clutter
* Keep your doors and windows locked when not in use
* Post security signs to deter intruders
* Consider investing in a home security system
* Stay informed about local crime trends and take necessary precautions

### **Best Practices:**

* Keep a record of all entry and exit points
* Install security cameras in high-traffic areas
* Use motion-sensing lights to improve security
* Consider investing in smart home devices
* Stay vigilant and regularly inspect your home's security measures

In conclusion, there are many budget-friendly home security upgrades you can do on your own to enhance the safety of your home. From DIY projects to smart locks and security cameras, we've explored 5 simple and affordable ways to boost your home's security. By following these tips and best practices, you can create a safe and secure home for you and your family.
