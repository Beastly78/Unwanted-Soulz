**Affordable Motorcycle Upgrades for Beginners: Upgrade Your Ride Without Breaking the Bank**

**Section 1: Lighting Upgrades**
================================

A well-lit motorcycle is not only more noticeable on the road, but it's also safer for both you and other drivers. Upgrading your lighting system can be a simple and cost-effective way to enhance your bike's visibility.

### Why Upgrade Your Lighting?

* Improved visibility for you and other drivers
* Enhanced safety on the road, especially during nighttime riding
* Customizable designs to match your bike's style

### Affordable Lighting Upgrade Options

#### **Option 1: LED Headlight Upgrade**

* **Product:** LED Headlight Kit by Cateye ($20-$50)
* **Pros:**
	+ Increased visibility and safety
	+ Long-lasting and energy-efficient LEDs

* **Cons:**

	+ Some users report flickering or uneven illumination

#### **Option 2: LED Turn Signal Upgrade**
