**Protect Your Home Without Breaking the Bank: Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do**

Are you worried about the safety and security of your home, but don't want to break the bank on expensive upgrades? You're not alone. **Section 1: Lighting Up Security with DIY Projects**
------------------------------------------------

DIY projects are an excellent way to upgrade your home's security without spending a lot of money. One of the simplest and most effective DIY projects is to install motion-sensitive outdoor lights. These lights can deter intruders and improve visibility around your home, making it harder for burglars to sneak in undetected.

### Product Recommendation: GE Ultra-Bright LED Motion-Sensing Spotlights

*   **Price:** $25-$35
*   **Pros:**
    - Energy-efficient LED bulbs
    - Long-lasting and durable design
    - Weather-resistant for outdoor use
*   **Cons:**
    - May not be as effective in heavy rain or snow
    - Battery life may vary depending on usage

Another DIY project is to install security cameras using old phones. Many people throw away their old phones, but these devices can be repurposed as security cameras. Simply install a camera app, mount the phone in a strategic location, and you'll have a low-cost security camera system.

### Product Recommendation: Stick Up Cam Battery by Ring

*   **Price:** $99-$149
*   **Pros:**
    - Easy to install and move around
    - Motion detection and alerts for suspicious activity
    - Weather-resistant for outdoor use
*   **Cons:**
    - Limited storage capacity for video recordings
    - Requires a monthly subscription for cloud storage
