**Section 1: Why Safety Matters**
------------------------------------

Before we dive into the recommended helmets, it's essential to understand why safety is crucial when it comes to motorcycle riding. A helmet can make all the difference in preventing head injuries and fatalities in the event of an accident.

According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), helmets can reduce the risk of head injury by up to 70% and fatality by up to 37%. With these statistics in mind, it's clear that investing in a good quality helmet is a no-brainer.

**Section 2: What to Look for in a Helmet**
---------------------------------------------

When searching for the best motorcycle helmets under $200, there are several factors to consider. Here are some key features to look for:

* **ANSI, DOT, or Snell Certification**: Ensure that your helmet meets the safety standards set by reputable organizations such as the American National Standards Institute (ANSI), Department of Transportation (DOT), or Snell Memorial Foundation.
* **Comfort**: Look for a helmet with a snug fit, breathable padding, and a comfortable design that won't cause neck strain.
* **Visor or Face Shield**: Consider a helmet with a built-in visor or face shield to protect your face from wind, debris, and UV rays.
* **Ventilation**: Proper ventilation is crucial for preventing heat buildup and discomfort. Look for a helmet with adjustable vents and a moisture-wicking liner.
* **Style**: While safety should be your top priority, it's also essential to find a helmet that fits your personal style.
