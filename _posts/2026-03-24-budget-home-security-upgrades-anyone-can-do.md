**Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do**
=====================================================

As a homeowner, you want to ensure that your family's safety and security are protected at all times. However, purchasing an expensive home security system can be a significant financial burden for many of us. Fortunately, there are budget-friendly home security upgrades that anyone can do to enhance their home's security without breaking the bank.

In this article, we'll explore five budget-friendly home security upgrades that you can do to improve your home's security, along with some product recommendations and their pros and cons.

**Upgrade 1: Smart Lighting**

Smart lighting is one of the simplest and most effective home security upgrades that you can do. Smart lighting systems use motion sensors and wireless connectivity to provide bright lights to deter potential intruders and make it easier for you to see what's going on outside your home.

**Why Smart Lighting Matters**

Smart lighting can serve as a visual deterrent, making it clear that your home is being watched and monitored. Additionally, smart lighting systems can alert you to any unusual activity outside your home, allowing you to respond quickly and prevent potential break-ins.

**Product Recommendation #1: Ring Stick Up Cam**

The Ring Stick Up Cam is a budget-friendly smart lighting solution that can be battery-powered or hardwired to a power source. It features motion detection, LED spotlights, and two-way audio capabilities for real-time communication.

**Pros:**

* Affordable price point (starting at $100)
* Easy to install and move around
* Battery life up to 6 months on a single charge
* 1080p video resolution for clear footage

**Cons:**

* Limited range of motion detection (up to 10 feet)
* No built-in siren or alarm system

**Other Options:**

* LIFX Wi-Fi Smart LED Bulb (starter pack: $25-$50)
* Philips Hue Smart Lighting Starter Kit (starting at $50)

**Upgrade 2: Outdoor Security Cameras**

Outdoor security cameras are another essential home security upgrade that can provide a clear view of your property's perimeter. Wireless and waterproof cameras can be easily installed in strategic locations around your home to deter potential intruders and capture evidence in the event of a break-in.

**Why Outdoor Security Cameras Matter**

Outdoor security cameras can provide a bird's-eye view of your property, helping you keep an eye on suspicious activity and prevent potential break-ins. Some outdoor security cameras also come equipped with features like night vision and motion detection, making it easier to catch and deter intruders.

**Product Recommendation #2: Wyze Outdoor Camera**

The Wyze Outdoor Camera is a budget-friendly outdoor security camera solution that comes with 2MP video quality, night vision, and motion detection. It's also weather-resistant, making it perfect for exposure to the elements.

**Pros:**

* Extremely affordable price point (starting at $35)
* 2MP video resolution for clear footage
* Weather-resistant and durable
* Motion detection and alerts to your phone

**Cons:**

* No built-in siren or alarm system
* Limited storage capacity (requires cloud subscription for extended storage)

**Other Options:**

* Blink XT2 Outdoor Security Camera (starting at $35)
* Arlo Pro 3 Floodlight Camera (starting at $50)

**Upgrade 3: Smart Door Locks**

Smart door locks are another home security upgrade that can provide an additional layer of protection against unwanted guests and potential intruders. Smart locks can be easily integrated with your home security system or smartphone app, allowing you to control access and receive notifications remotely.

**Why Smart Door Locks Matter**

Smart door locks can provide an additional layer of protection against key duplication and access by unauthorized individuals. Additionally, smart door locks can allow you to grant access to trusted guests and service providers without having to physically hand over keys.

**Product Recommendation #3: Schlage Sense Smart Deadbolt**

The Schlage Sense Smart Deadbolt is a budget-friendly smart door lock solution that comes with Wi-Fi connectivity, Bluetooth, and mobile app control. It also features a touchscreen keypad for easy keyless entry.

**Pros:**

* Affordable price point (starting at $150)
* Easy to install and program
* Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connectivity for seamless integration
* Touchscreen keypad for easy keyless entry

**Cons:**

* Limited compatibility with certain smartphones (requires specific firmware)
* No built-in voice assistant integration

**Other Options:**

* August Smart Lock (starting at $100)
* Yale Assure Lock SL (starting at $200)

**Upgrade 4: Motion-Activated Sirens**

Motion-activated sirens are another effective home security upgrade that can deter potential intruders and alert neighbors in case of a break-in. These sirens can be placed strategically around your home to create a network of alerting devices.

**Why Motion-Activated Sirens Matter**

Motion-activated sirens can provide an immediate deterrent to potential intruders, making it easier for you to respond quickly and prevent a potential break-in. Additionally, these sirens can alert neighbors and law enforcement to the presence of suspicious activity.

**Upgrade 5: Home Security Signs and Decals**

Home security signs and decals are another budget-friendly home security upgrade that can serve as a visual deterrent to potential intruders. These signs and decals can be placed in visible areas around your home to signal that your home is being watched and monitored.

**Why Home Security Signs and Decals Matter**

Home security signs and decals can provide a clear visual deterrent to potential intruders, making it clear that your home is being watched and monitored. Additionally, these signs and decals can help alert the police and first responders in case of an emergency.
