**Protect Your Head, Protect Your Ride: Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200**
====================================================================================

As a motorcyclist, you know how essential it is to wear a helmet while riding. Not only does it protect your head in the event of an accident, but it also reduces the risk of head injury and death. However, with so many helmets available on the market, choosing the right one can be overwhelming. In this article, we'll explore the best motorcycle helmets under $200, highlighting their features, pros, and cons to help you make an informed decision.

**Section 1: Why a Helmet is a Must-Have**
-----------------------------------------

Before we dive into the best helmets under $200, let's talk about why a helmet is a must-have for motorcyclists. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), helmets can reduce the risk of head injury by 69% and the risk of fatality by 37% in the event of a motorcycle crash (NHTSA, 2020). Furthermore, a helmet can also protect your face and eyes from road debris and wind.

**Section 2: What to Look for in a Helmet**
------------------------------------------

When shopping for a helmet, consider the following factors:

* **Certification**: Look for a helmet that meets or exceeds safety standards set by organizations such as the Snell Memorial Foundation or the Department of Transportation (DOT).
* **Comfort**: A comfortable helmet is essential for long rides. Consider a helmet with padding and an adjustable fit system.
* **Visibility**: A clear visor or face shield is crucial for visibility. Look for a helmet with a quick-release mechanism to make it easy to open and close.
* **Weight**: Lighter helmets are easier to wear, but may not be as durable.

**Section 4: Additional Considerations**
--------------------------------------

When choosing a helmet, consider the following additional factors:

* **Sizing**: Make sure to try on a helmet before buying to ensure a comfortable fit.
* **Maintenance**: Regularly clean and maintain your helmet to ensure its longevity.
* **Accessories**: Consider additional accessories such as a helmet bag, pad set, or chin strap to enhance your riding experience.
