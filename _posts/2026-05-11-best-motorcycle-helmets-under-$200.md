# Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200: A Comprehensive Guide

## Introduction

When it comes to riding a motorcycle, safety should be your top priority. One of the most critical components of motorcycle safety gear is a high-quality helmet. Not only does a helmet protect your skull from potential impacts, but it also helps reduce wind noise and provides some level of protection from the elements.

However, not all helmets are created equal, and the cost can vary significantly from under $100 to over $1,000. In this article, we'll focus on the best motorcycle helmets available under $200. Whether you're a seasoned rider or just starting out, we'll help you find the perfect helmet for your budget and needs.

## Types of Motorcycle Helmets

Before we dive into our top recommendations, let's take a brief look at the different types of motorcycle helmets available:

* **Full-Face Helmets**: These are the safest type of helmet, covering the entire head and face.
* **Half-Helmets**: Also known as brain buckets, these helmets only cover the top of the head.
* **Open-Face Helmets**: These helmets offer protection for the head but leave the face exposed.
* **Off-Road/Adventure Helmets**: Designed for off-road and adventure riding, these helmets often feature a more aggressive design and more protective features.

For this article, we'll be focusing on full-face helmets, as they provide the best protection for the face and head.

## Section 1: Safety Features to Consider

When shopping for a motorcycle helmet, there are several safety features to consider:

* **Snell Certification**: This is a gold standard certification that ensures the helmet meets rigorous safety standards.
* **Impact Protection**: Look for helmets with multi-impact protection, which can withstand repeated impacts without losing their protective abilities.
* **Visor Protection**: A good visor should provide protection from wind, rain, and debris.
* **Certification for Specific Riding Styles**: If you'll be engaging in specific riding styles, such as off-road or racing, look for certifications that indicate the helmet is designed for that purpose.

## Section 2: Materials and Design

When it comes to materials and design, there are also several factors to consider:

* **Shell Material**: Look for helmets made from high-quality materials, such as polycarbonate or Kevlar.
* **Padding and Liners**: A good helmet should have comfortable and breathable padding and liners.
* **Weight**: Lighter helmets are often easier to wear, especially on long rides.
* **Ventilation**: Good ventilation can help keep you cool and comfortable during long rides.
