**Section 1: Introduction to Motorcycle Helmets**

Before we dive into the best motorcycle helmets under $200, let's take a look at the importance of wearing a helmet while biking. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), wearing a helmet can reduce the risk of head injury by up to 70% and the risk of death by up to 37%. With these statistics in mind, it's clear that investing in a good helmet is a crucial aspect of motorcycle safety.

**Section 2: Factors to Consider When Buying a Motorcycle Helmet**

When shopping for a motorcycle helmet, there are several factors to consider. Here are some of the key considerations:

* **Safety Rating**: Look for helmets that have been certified by reputable safety organizations such as the Department of Transportation (DOT) or the Snell Memorial Foundation.
* **Fit**: Ensure that the helmet fits snugly and comfortably, with adequate ventilation to prevent overheating.
* **Comfort**: Consider the type of padding and the weight of the helmet, as well as the presence of visor, sun visor, or breath guard.
* **Visibility**: Opt for a helmet with clear visibility, allowing you to see the road and surroundings without obstruction.
* **Price**: Set a budget and stick to it, as high-end helmets can be quite expensive.

**Section 4: Additional Features to Consider**

In addition to the factors mentioned earlier, here are some additional features to consider when purchasing a motorcycle helmet:
