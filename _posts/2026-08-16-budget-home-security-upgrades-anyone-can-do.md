**Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do**

Are you tired of worrying about the safety and security of your home? Do you want to upgrade your home's security without breaking the bank? **Section 1: Improving Outdoor Lighting**

Outdoor lighting is one of the simplest and most effective ways to improve home security. Motion-sensitive lights can deter intruders and alert you to potential threats. You can install motion-sensitive lights around your home's perimeter, including the front porch, back door, and any other areas that are vulnerable to trespassing.

**Section 2: Smart Locks and Secure Entry Points**

Smart locks are a great way to improve home security by providing a secure and convenient way to enter and exit your home. You can install a smart lock on your front door, back door, or any other entry point that you want to secure. Smart locks can be controlled remotely through a mobile app, allowing you to grant access to guests or contractors and track who's entering and exiting your home.
