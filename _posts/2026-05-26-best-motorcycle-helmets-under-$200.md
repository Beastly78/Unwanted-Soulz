# **Best Motorcycle Helmets under $200: Protection Without Breaking the Bank**

When it comes to motorcycle safety, one of the most crucial pieces of gear is a high-quality helmet. Not only does it protect your head in the event of a crash, but it also provides comfort and style on the road. However, choosing the right helmet can be overwhelming, especially on a budget. In this article, we'll explore the best motorcycle helmets under $200, so you can ride with confidence without breaking the bank.

## **Section 1: What to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**

Before we dive into our top picks, let's discuss what you should look for in a motorcycle helmet. Here are some key factors to consider:

- **Safety certification**: Look for helmets that meet or exceed safety standards set by organizations like the Department of Transportation (DOT) or the Snell Memorial Foundation.
- **Fit**: The helmet should fit snugly but not too tightly, providing a comfortable fit for long rides.
- **Visor**: A clear visor with good UV protection is essential for visibility and eye protection.
- **Ventilation**: Adequate ventilation is crucial for airflow and temperature regulation.
- **Weight**: A lighter helmet is easier to manage, especially for longer rides.
