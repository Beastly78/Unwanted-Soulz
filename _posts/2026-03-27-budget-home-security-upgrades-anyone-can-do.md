**Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do**
=====================================================

**Introduction**
---------------

As the threat of home security breaches and burglaries continues to rise, many homeowners find themselves worried about the safety and security of their family and property. However, investing in high-end home security systems can be expensive, and sometimes out of reach for many families. But fear not! There are several budget-friendly home security upgrades that anyone can do to enhance the safety and security of their home without breaking the bank.

In this article, we will explore 5 simple and affordable home security upgrades that you can do with minimal effort and cost. These upgrades will help you deter burglars, alert authorities in case of a break-in, and even keep an eye on your home from afar. So, whether you're a homeowner, renter, or DIY enthusiast, these budget-friendly solutions are perfect for anyone looking to upgrade their home security.

**Section 1: Motion-Sensor Outdoor Lighting**
--------------------------------------------

	+ Cons: Limited night vision, some users report false triggers
* **Ring Floodlight Cam**: This wireless floodlight camera has a 160-degree field of view, 1080p HD video, and a 4-second delay. It also has a built-in siren and Wi-Fi connectivity. ($129-$149)
	+ Pros: Bright floodlight, long battery life, weather-resistant
	+ Cons: Some users report false triggers, limited night vision

**Section 5: DIY Home Security Tips**
----------------------------------

In addition to these budget-friendly upgrades, here are some simple DIY home security tips to keep in mind:

* **Post signs**: Post "No Trespassing" or "Beware of the Dog" signs to deter potential burglars.
* **Light up your yard**: Use solar-powered lights to illuminate potential entry points and keep your yard visible.
* **Secure your doors**: Use a reliable deadbolt lock and secure your doors with a doorstop or a wooden wedge.
* **Keep valuables out of sight**: Avoid leaving valuables such as laptops, cash, or jewelry in plain sight.
