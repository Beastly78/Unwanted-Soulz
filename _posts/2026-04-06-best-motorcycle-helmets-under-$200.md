**Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200: Protect Your Head in Style**

As a motorcyclist, you know the importance of safety gear. Wearing a helmet is crucial to preventing head injuries and fatalities in the event of an accident. ### Top Factors to Consider When Buying a Helmet

When shopping for a helmet, there are several factors to consider, including:

* **Snell or DOT certification**: Look for helmets that meet either Snell or DOT certification standards, which ensure that the helmet meets minimum safety requirements.
* **Fit and comfort**: Choose a helmet that fits your head comfortably, with adjustable padding to ensure a snug fit.
* **Shield and visor**: Consider a helmet with a removable and washable shield or visor for easy maintenance.
* **Ventilation**: Opt for a helmet with good ventilation to keep your head cool during long rides.
* **Weight**: Lighter helmets are easier to wear, but may compromise on safety features.
