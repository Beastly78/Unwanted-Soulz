**Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200: A Buyer's Guide**

Whether you're an experienced motorcyclist or a newcomer to the world of biking, safety should always be your top priority. One of the most crucial elements of motorcycling safety is the helmet you wear. A good helmet can protect your head from severe injuries or even save your life in the event of an accident. However, a high-quality motorcycle helmet doesn't have to break the bank. In this article, we'll explore the best motorcycle helmets under $200 that offer excellent protection, comfort, and affordability.

**Types of Helmets: What to Expect**

Before we dive into the recommendations, it's essential to understand the different types of motorcycle helmets available on the market. Here are the most common types of helmets, each with its unique characteristics:

* **Full-face helmets**: These helmets provide the most comprehensive protection for your face and head. They have a chin bar that covers your mouth and nose, and a visor that shields your eyes from the sun.
* **Open-face helmets**: These helmets are similar to full-face helmets but don't have a chin bar. They offer less protection, but are a popular choice for casual bikers who prioritize comfort and ventilation.
* **Half-helmets**: These helmets cover only the top of your head and are ideal for short trips or casual cruising.

**Section 1: Helmets with Safety Features**

When shopping for a motorcycle helmet under $200, look for safety features that meet or exceed the standards set by the U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) or the Snell Memorial Foundation. Some notable safety features to consider include:

* **Impact absorption**: A helmet with a thick, durable shell is crucial for absorbing impact in the event of a crash.
* **Face shield**: A face shield or visor should be shatter-resistant and provide adequate ventilation to prevent fogging.
* **Quick release**: A quick-release Chin strap or buckle can save you precious seconds in an emergency.

**Section 3: Safety Standards and Certifications**

When shopping for a motorcycle helmet, ensure it meets or exceeds the safety standards set by reputable organizations. Here are the most popular standards:

* **DOT (U.S. Department of Transportation)**: The standard for motorcycle helmets in the United States.
* **Snell Memorial Foundation**: A non-profit organization that sets and maintains voluntary standards for motorcycle helmets.
* **ECE (Economic Commission for Europe)**: A standard for motorcycle helmets in Europe and other countries.

**Section 4: Comfort and Fit**

A comfortable and snug fit is crucial for an enjoyable motorcycling experience. Here are some essential features to consider:

* **Linings and padding**: Ensure the helmet has a breathable and comfortable lining that won't cause irritation or discomfort.
* **Adjustable straps**: Look for helmets with adjustable straps to accommodate different head shapes and sizes.
* **Weight**: A lightweight helmet will reduce fatigue and discomfort during long rides.

**Section 5: Maintenance and Care**

To ensure your motorcycle helmet lasts for years to come, follow these maintenance tips:

* **Regular cleaning**: Use a soft cloth and mild soap to clean your helmet.
* **Avoid extreme temperatures**: Store your helmet away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures.
* **Replace regularly**: Replace your helmet every 3-5 years or if you notice significant wear and tear.
