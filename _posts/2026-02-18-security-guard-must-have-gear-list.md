**Protecting People, Property, and Perimeters: A Security Guard's Equipment Essentials**

* **Cons:**

The ACR ResQLink+ is a lightweight and compact PLB perfect for security guards working in remote areas. Its waterproof and buoyant design ensures it remains operational even in the most extreme conditions.
