**Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200: A Comprehensive Review**

Are you in the market for a new motorcycle helmet but don't want to break the bank? Look no further. In this article, we'll explore the best motorcycle helmets available for under $200. From safety features to style, we'll cover everything you need to know to make an informed decision.

**Section 1: The Importance of Wearing a Helmet**

When it comes to motorcycle riding, one of the most essential pieces of gear is a helmet. Not only does a helmet protect your head in the event of an accident, but it also provides comfort and confidence on the road. But with so many options available, it can be overwhelming to choose the right one. That's why we're here to help.

According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), helmets reduce the risk of head injury by 69% and fatal head injury by 37%. This is a staggering statistic, and it highlights the importance of wearing a helmet while riding.

**Section 2: Features to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**

Before we dive into our top picks, it's essential to understand what features to look for in a motorcycle helmet. Here are some key factors to consider:

* **Safety rating:** Look for a helmet with a Snell or DOT rating. These ratings ensure that the helmet meets minimum safety standards.
* **Comfort:** Choose a helmet with a comfortable fit, including a secure retention system and adequate ventilation.
* **Visibility:** Consider a helmet with a clear visor to improve visibility while riding.
* **Style:** Motorcycle helmets come in a variety of styles, from aggressive to sporty.
* **Noise level:** A lower noise level can help reduce fatigue and improve overall riding experience.

**Section 4: Additional Tips**

When selecting a motorcycle helmet, consider the following additional tips:

* **Try before you buy:** If possible, try on different helmets before purchasing to ensure a comfortable fit.
* **Check the warranty:** Look for a manufacturer that offers a comprehensive warranty and excellent customer service.
* **Consider the materials:** Look for helmets made from durable materials, such as ABS or polycarbonate.
* **Check for DOT or Snell ratings:** Ensure that the helmet meets minimum safety standards.
