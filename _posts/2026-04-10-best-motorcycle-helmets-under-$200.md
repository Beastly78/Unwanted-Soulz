**Section 1: Importance of Wearing a Helmet**
--------------------------------------------

Before we dive into the best motorcycle helmets under $200, it's essential to emphasize the importance of wearing a helmet while riding. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), motorcycle helmets are 37% more likely to reduce the risk of fatality in motorcycle crashes. In addition, helmets help reduce the risk of head injuries, facial injuries, and fatalities. In short, a helmet is not just a riding gear, it's a lifesaver.

**Section 2: Factors to Consider When Choosing a Helmet**
---------------------------------------------------

Before buying a helmet, consider the following factors:

* **Size:** Ensure the helmet fits comfortably to avoid any distractions or discomfort while riding.
* **Weight:** Lighter helmets are more comfortable to wear and reduce fatigue.
* **Visor:** A good visor helps reduce glare and provides clear visibility.
* **Ventilation:** Good ventilation helps regulate temperature and prevent fogging.
* **Noise reduction:** Ear protection is crucial for safe riding.
* **Certification:** Ensure the helmet meets safety standards such as DOT, Snell, or ECE certifications.
