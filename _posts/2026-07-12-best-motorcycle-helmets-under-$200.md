**Section 1: Why a Good Helmet Matters**

When it comes to motorcycle riding, a good helmet is essential. Not only does it protect your head from impact in the event of an accident, but it also regulates temperature, reduces wind noise, and provides comfort during long rides. A cheap helmet might seem like a good option, but it can compromise on safety and quality. A helmet that costs under $200 can provide excellent protection without breaking the bank.

**Section 2: Safety Features to Look For**

When searching for a motorcycle helmet under $200, there are several safety features to look out for:

* **DOT Certification**: Look for helmets that meet the U.S. Department of Transportation's (DOT) safety standard for motorcycle helmets. This ensures that the helmet meets minimum safety requirements.
* **Snell Certification**: The Snell Memorial Foundation is a non-profit organization that sets standards for helmet safety. Helmets that meet Snell certification have undergone rigorous testing and offer superior protection.
* **Impact Protection**: Choose a helmet with a multi-layered construction to absorb impact energy and reduce the force of a crash.
* **Eyeport and Chin Strap**: Ensure the helmet has an eyeport that allows for a clear view while maintaining safety, and a chin strap that securely fastens to the head.

**Section 4: Additional Safety Tips**

In addition to choosing a helmet under $200 that meets safety standards, here are some additional safety tips to consider:

* **Always wear a helmet while riding**: This is the most crucial safety tip. Never ride without a helmet.
* **Adjust the helmet correctly**: Ensure the helmet fits snugly, with the visor aligned properly.
* **Replace the helmet every 3-5 years**: Helmets have a lifespan and can degrade over time, compromising their safety.
* **Practice proper riding techniques**: Stay alert, use hand signals, and follow traffic laws.
