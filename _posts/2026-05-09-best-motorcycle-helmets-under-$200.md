**Section 1: Why Wear a Helmet?**
=====================================

Motorcycle helmets are a crucial aspect of any rider's safety equipment. Not only do they protect your head from impact in the event of a crash, but they also provide protection from the elements, wind, and debris. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), helmets can reduce the risk of head injury by up to 37% and fatal head injuries by up to 70%.

**Section 2: Types of Motorcycle Helmets**
=========================================

When shopping for a motorcycle helmet, there are several types to consider:

* **Full-Face Helmets**: These helmets cover the entire head, offering maximum protection for your face and brain.
* **Half-Face Helmets**: These helmets cover the top and sides of the head, leaving the face exposed.
* **Open-Face Helmets**: These helmets cover the top of the head, with an open face to allow for a clear view.

**Section 3: What to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**
======================================================

When shopping for a motorcycle helmet under $200, consider the following factors:

* **Shells**: Look for a helmet with a durable, UV-stable shell that will protect your head from impact.
* **Visors**: A good visor should provide clear visibility, be easy to clean, and have a secure attachment.
* **Padding and Comfort**: Opt for a helmet with breathable padding and a secure fit.
* **Certification**: Ensure the helmet meets or exceeds the safety standards set by organizations such as the Department of Transportation (DOT) or Snell Memorial Foundation.
