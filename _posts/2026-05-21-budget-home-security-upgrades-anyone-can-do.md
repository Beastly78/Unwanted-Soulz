**Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do**

As a homeowner, you're no doubt aware of the importance of home security. However, many of us may feel that upgrading our home security systems is out of our budget. The good news is that there are many simple and affordable ways to boost your home's security without breaking the bank.

In this article, we'll explore five budget-friendly home security upgrades that anyone can do. Plus, we'll highlight three top product recommendations and their pros and cons. Whether you're a DIY enthusiast or just looking for ways to secure your home on a budget, this article has got you covered.

### Upgrade 1: Smart Lighting

Smart lighting is a fantastic way to enhance your home's security without spending a fortune. By installing smart light bulbs, you can:

* Illuminate areas of your home that are prone to crime, such as your front porch or backyard
* Create the illusion of occupancy when you're away, deterring potential burglars
* Adjust the brightness and color of your lights remotely, making it easier to keep an eye on your home

Some popular smart lighting options include:

**Recommendation 1: Philips Hue White and Color Starter Kit**

* **Pros:**
 + Easy to install and use
 + Bright and vibrant colors
 + Compatible with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant
* **Cons:**
 + More expensive than some other smart lighting options
 + Requires a hub to connect to Wi-Fi

The Philips Hue White and Color Starter Kit is a great option for anyone looking to upgrade their home's lighting. With 2 bulbs and a hub included, you'll be able to create a variety of bright and colorful effects to help enhance your home's security.

### Upgrade 2: Door and Window Sensors

Doors and windows are the most common entry points for burglars. Upgrading your door and window sensors is a simple and affordable way to boost your home's security. These sensors:

* Alert you to open or closed doors and windows
* Allow you to check the status of your doors and windows remotely
* Can be integrated with other smart home devices for a more comprehensive security system

Some popular door and window sensor options include:

**Recommendation 2: Schlage Sense Smart Deadbolt**

* **Pros:**
 + Easy to install and use
 + Compatible with Apple HomeKit and Samsung SmartThings
 + Keyless entry option
* **Cons:**
 + Requires a hub to pair with other smart devices
 + Limited smart home functionality compared to some other door sensors

The Schlage Sense Smart Deadbolt is a top-notch option for door sensors. With keyless entry and seamless integration with popular smart home ecosystems, this sensor is a great choice for anyone looking to upgrade their home's security.

### Upgrade 3: Outdoor Security Cameras

Outdoor security cameras are a must-have for any home security system. These cameras:

* Provide a clear view of your home's exterior
* Alert you to potential intruders
* Can be used to monitor other areas of your property, such as a pool or shed

Some popular outdoor security camera options include:

**Recommendation 3: Reolink Argus 3 Pro**

* **Pros:**
 + Waterproof and weather-resistant
 + Wireless and easy to install
 + Motion detection and alerts
* **Cons:**
 + Night vision may not be as clear as other cameras
 + Requires a subscription for full functionality

The Reolink Argus 3 Pro is a top-notch option for outdoor security cameras. With its waterproof design and motion detection capabilities, this camera is a great choice for anyone looking to secure their home's exterior.
