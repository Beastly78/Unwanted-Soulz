**Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do**
======================================================

As a homeowner, the safety and security of your property are likely at the top of your list of concerns. A home security system can be a significant investment, but you don't have to break the bank to feel secure in your own home. With a few simple upgrades, you can significantly improve the security of your home without sacrificing your budget.

In this article, we'll explore five budget-friendly home security upgrades that anyone can do, including product recommendations and a list of pros and cons for each. Whether you're a homeowner, renter, or DIY enthusiast, these upgrades will provide you with the peace of mind that comes with knowing your home is secure.

**Section 1: Installing Smart Light Bulbs**
-----------------------------------------

One of the simplest and most effective ways to improve home security is by installing smart light bulbs. Smart light bulbs can be controlled remotely, making it appear as though someone is home even when you're not. This can be a powerful deterrent for potential intruders.

**How it works:** Smart light bulbs can be controlled using a smartphone app, allowing you to turn them on and off remotely. You can also set up schedules and custom lighting scenes to make it appear as though someone is home.

**Product Recommendations:**

* **Philips Hue White and Color Smart Light Bulb**: Philips Hue is a popular and affordable smart lighting option that can be controlled using the Philips Hue app. ($60/package of 4 bulbs)
* **Belkin Wemo Smart Light Bulb**: Belkin's Wemo smart lighting system allows you to control your lights remotely and set up custom schedules. ($20/bulb)

**Pros and Cons:**

| Product | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Philips Hue | Affordable, wide range of colors, easy to install | Requires hub to function, limited color options with some products |
| Belkin Wemo | Easy to install, affordable, wide range of products | Limited color options, requires internet connection to function |

**Section 2: Installing Motion-Activated Outdoor Lights**
---------------------------------------------------

Motion-activated outdoor lights are another effective way to improve home security. These lights can be placed around your home's perimeter and will turn on automatically when motion is detected.

**How it works:** Motion-activated outdoor lights use sensors to detect movement and turn on when motion is detected. Some models also have timers and motion sensitivity settings, allowing you to customize the lights' behavior.

**Product Recommendations:**

* **Ring Stick Up Cam**: The Ring Stick Up Cam is a battery-powered motion-activated camera that can also serve as an outdoor light. ($100/camera)
* **Sylvania Smart Motion Outdoor Light**: The Sylvania Smart Motion Outdoor Light is a motion-activated light that can be controlled remotely and has a wide range of color options. ($50/light)

**Pros and Cons:**

| Product | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ring Stick Up Cam | Affordable, wide range of cameras available, easy to install | Limited battery life, requires internet connection to function |
| Sylvania Smart Motion Outdoor Light | Easy to install, affordable, wide range of color options | Limited motion detection range, requires internet connection to function |

**Section 3: Installing a Door Sensor**
--------------------------------------

A door sensor is a simple and effective way to improve home security. These sensors can detect when a door is opened or closed and alert you via a smartphone app.

**How it works:** Door sensors use a magnet and sensor to detect when a door is opened or closed. Some models also have features such as door opening detection and custom alerts.

**Product Recommendations:**

* **August Smart Lock**: The August Smart Lock is a smart door lock that also includes a door sensor. ($70/lock)
* **Wyze Wireless Door Sensor**: The Wyze Wireless Door Sensor is a battery-powered door sensor that can also be used with the Wyze smart home system. ($20/sensor)

**Pros and Cons:**

| Product | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| August Smart Lock | Easy to install, wide range of features, affordable | Limited customization options, requires internet connection to function |
| Wyze Wireless Door Sensor | Affordable, easy to install, battery-powered | Limited features compared to other products, limited compatibility with smart home systems |
