**Protect Your Head: Top 5 Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200**

Hitting the open road on two wheels can be exhilarating, but safety should always be your top priority.  A motorcycle helmet is more than just a fashion accessory, it's a crucial safety device that can literally save your life in the event of a crash. When shopping for a helmet, it's essential to find one that not only fits comfortably but also meets the necessary safety standards.

The truth is, many motorcycle riders assume that the more expensive the helmet, the safer it is. But the reality is that a high-end helmet may offer superior features, but it doesn't necessarily guarantee better protection. So, how do you find a budget-friendly motorcycle helmet that won't break the bank? Look no further! In this article, we'll explore the best motorcycle helmets under $200 that offer an excellent combination of safety, style, and affordability.

### **Safety First: What to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**

Before we dive into our top picks, let's discuss the essential features to look for in a motorcycle helmet:

* **Safety certification:** Make sure the helmet meets either U.S. Department of Transportation (DOT) or Snell Memorial Foundation standards. These organizations evaluate helmets based on their ability to absorb impact and protect the rider's head.
* **Shell material:** A sturdy outer shell is critical for absorbing impact. Look for helmets made from high-quality materials like polycarbonate or Kevlar.
* **Face shield:** A detachable and adjustable face shield is essential for visibility and protection against wind, dust, and debris.
* **Linings and padding:** A comfortable and breathable lining can help prevent heat buildup and reduce fatigue during long rides.
* **Visibility:** Consider a helmet with a built-in visor or a face shield with a clear visibility zone.

With these safety features in mind, let's explore our top three picks for motorcycle helmets under $200.

### **Additional Tips for Choosing a Motorcycle Helmet**

While our top picks offer excellent value for money, there are several other factors to consider when selecting a motorcycle helmet:

* **Fit:** Make sure to try on several helmets and adjust the fit to your individual head shape.
* **Noise:** Look for helmets with built-in audio systems or consider an earplug design for increased hearing protection.
* **Goggle compatibility:** Consider a helmet with a built-in visor or a compatible goggle system.
* **Maintenance:** Regularly inspect and maintain your helmet to ensure continued safety and performance.

By considering these factors and our top three picks, you'll be well on your way to finding a safe and stylish motorcycle helmet that won't break the bank.
