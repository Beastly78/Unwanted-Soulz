# Budget Home Security Upgrades Anyone Can Do
## Secure Your Home Without Breaking the Bank

As a homeowner, it's natural to feel concerned about the safety and security of your property. Breaking and entering, theft, and even vandalism can happen to anyone, anywhere. However, upgrading your home security doesn't have to be expensive. With a few DIY upgrades and affordable products, you can enhance the safety of your home without breaking the bank.

In this article, we'll explore five budget-friendly home security upgrades anyone can do. From smart doorbells to motion-sensitive lights, we'll cover the essentials to protect your home and family. We'll also review three affordable security products that are worth considering for your home security upgrade.

## Section 1: Assess Your Home Security
Before making any upgrades, take a closer look at your home's current security features. Consider the following questions:

* Do you have any existing security cameras or motion-sensitive lights?
* Are your doors and windows secure (locks, deadbolts, etc.)?
* Do you have a home alarm system (if so, is it working properly)?

Answering these questions will help you identify areas for improvement and prioritize your upgrades.

## Section 2: Smart Doorbells for Enhanced Security
A smart doorbell is a great place to start your home security upgrades. These devices not only allow you to see who's at the door from anywhere in the world but also often come with advanced features like motion detection, night vision, and two-way audio. Some popular smart doorbell options include:

### Product Recommendation 1: Ring Video Doorbell
**Pros:**

* 1080p video quality
* Motion detection and alerts
* Two-way audio and live streaming
* Wi-Fi connectivity
* Battery-powered (requires recharging every 6 months)

**Cons:**

* Requires subscription to Ring Protect Plus ($10/month) for cloud storage
* Limited range of motion detection (about 30 ft)
* Some users report issues with battery life and charging

### Recommendation 2: Nest Hello Video Doorbell
**Pros:**

* 4-star resolution video quality
* Motion detection and alerts
* Two-way audio and live streaming
* Integrated with Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa
* More comprehensive range of motion detection (about 60 ft)

**Cons:**

* More expensive than Ring Video Doorbell ($229-$299)
* Requires subscription to Nest Aware ($6-20/month) for cloud storage and additional features
* Some users report issues with doorbell's power requirements and Wi-Fi connectivity

### Recommendation 3: Arlo Pro 3 Wi-Fi Doorbell
**Pros:**

* 2K video quality
* Motion detection and alerts
* Two-way audio and live streaming
* Weather-resistant and vandal-resistant
* 6 months of free cloud storage

**Cons:**

* More expensive than Ring Video Doorbell ($199-$299)
* Requires subscription to Arlo Smart ($4-15/month) for advanced features and cloud storage
* Some users report issues with Wi-Fi connectivity and battery life

## Section 3: Motion-Sensitive Lights for Deterrence
Motion-sensitive lights are another excellent security upgrade for your home. These lights can be set up to turn on automatically when they detect motion, effectively scaring off potential intruders. Some popular options include:

* Solar-powered lights with motion sensors
* LED floodlights with built-in motion sensors
* Smart lighting systems with motion detection integration

When choosing motion-sensitive lights, consider factors like brightness, coverage area, and battery life.

## Section 4: Smart Locks for Enhanced Door Security
Smart locks can provide an extra layer of security for your home by allowing you to control access remotely. Some popular smart lock options include:

* Keyless entry locks with Wi-Fi connectivity
* August Smart Locks
* Schlage Sense Smart Deadbolt

When selecting a smart lock, consider factors like compatibility with existing door hardware, ease of use, and additional features like voice control.

## Section 5: Neighborhood Watch and Community Engagement
While individual security upgrades are essential, engaging with your community can also play a significant role in preventing crime. Consider the following:

* Organize a neighborhood watch program to keep an eye on each other's properties
* Attend local community meetings to discuss crime prevention and security concerns
* Get to know your neighbors to build trust and foster a sense of community

## Conclusion
Upgrading your home security doesn't have to break the bank. By following these budget-friendly tips and considering affordable security products like Ring Video Doorbell, Nest Hello Video Doorbell, and Arlo Pro 3 Wi-Fi Doorbell, you can effectively enhance the safety of your home. Remember to also engage with your community to create a secure and watchful neighborhood.

### Final Tips:

* Regularly review and update your home security plan to ensure it remains effective
* Consider consulting a security professional to assess your home's vulnerabilities and provide personalized recommendations
* Invest in a comprehensive security system or package deal for a discounted rate

By taking these steps, you can rest assured that your home and family are protected from potential threats.
