**Best Motorcycle Helmets Under $200: A Comprehensive Guide**
==============================================================

### Introduction

Riding a motorcycle can be an exhilarating experience, offering a sense of freedom and adventure that's hard to find on the highway. However, safety should always be the top priority, and a good quality helmet is essential for protecting your head in the event of an accident. But with so many options available, choosing the right helmet can be overwhelming, especially when you're on a budget. In this article, we'll help you navigate the world of motorcycle helmets under $200, highlighting the best options available.

**Section 1: Why You Need a Good Quality Helmet**
------------------------------------------------

A good quality helmet can make all the difference in the event of an accident. According to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA), wearing a helmet can reduce the risk of head injury by up to 70%. Furthermore, helmets that meet safety standards set by organizations like the Department of Transportation (DOT) and the Snell Memorial Foundation (Snell) are proven to provide superior protection.

### Safety Features to Look for in a Helmet

When shopping for a helmet, look for the following safety features:

* **Safety Certification**: Ensure that the helmet meets safety standards set by reputable organizations like DOT, Snell, or the European ECE certification.
* **Shell Material**: Opt for helmets made from high-quality materials like polycarbonate, ABS, or Kevlar.
* **Visor and Face Shield**: Choose a helmet with a shield that's secure, scratch-resistant, and provides excellent visibility.
* **Straps and Chin Strap**: Ensure that the helmet has adjustable straps and a chin strap that's secure and won't shift during a crash.

**Section 2: Types of Helmets Under $200**
------------------------------------------

Not all helmets are created equal, and the type you choose will depend on your riding style and preferences. Here are some common types of helmets available in the under-$200 price range:

* **Full-Face Helmets**: These helmets provide comprehensive protection, covering your entire head and neck.
* **Half-Face Helmets**: These helmets offer good protection for your face but leave your neck exposed.
* **Open-Face Helmets**: These helmets provide minimal protection and are often worn by riders who only ride short distances or in urban areas.
* **Modular Helmets**: These helmets have a removable chin bar and convert to an open-face helmet.
