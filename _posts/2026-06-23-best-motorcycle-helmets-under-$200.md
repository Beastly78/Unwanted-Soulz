**Section 1: What to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**

Before we dive into our top picks, let's talk about what to look for in a motorcycle helmet. Here are some key factors to consider:

* **Safety**: Look for helmets that meet or exceed safety standards set by the Department of Transportation (DOT) or the Snell Memorial Foundation.
* **Comfort**: Choose a helmet that fits comfortably and has adequate ventilation to keep you cool and dry.
* **Style**: Pick a helmet that fits your personal style and complements your motorcycle.
* **Price**: Of course, we're focusing on helmets under $200 in this article, but keep in mind that you get what you pay for.
