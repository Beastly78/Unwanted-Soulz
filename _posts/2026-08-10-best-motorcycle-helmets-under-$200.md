**Section 2: Features to Look for in a Motorcycle Helmet**

When shopping for a helmet, there are several features to consider. Here are some of the key things to look for:

* **Safety Ratings**: Look for helmets that meet or exceed the safety standards set by organizations such as the Department of Transportation (DOT) and the Snell Memorial Foundation.
* **Comfort**: A comfortable helmet is essential for long rides. Look for helmets with adjustable padding, ventilation systems, and a comfortable earcup design.
* **Weight**: A lighter helmet is easier to wear and reduces fatigue on long rides.
* **Visors**: A clear visor provides excellent visibility, but some riders prefer tinted visors for style and UV protection.
* **Shells**: Choose a helmet with a durable shell that provides excellent protection in the event of an accident.

By considering these features, you can find a helmet that meets your needs and provides the protection you need.

Here are some additional tips and considerations to keep in mind when shopping for a motorcycle helmet:
