**Section 1: Performance Upgrades**
-------------------------------

When it comes to performance upgrades, the key is to focus on areas that will provide the most significant impact on your bike's performance without breaking the bank. Here are some of the top upgrades to consider:

### 1. **Cold Air Intake**

* **What is it?**: A cold air intake system draws in cooler air from outside the engine compartment, resulting in a significant increase in horsepower and torque.
* **Pros:** Increased power, better gas mileage, and a more aggressive sound.
* **Cons:** May require additional tuning, can be noisy, and may not fit all bike models.

**Recommended Product:** **AEM Cold Air Intake System** ($150-$250)

* Pros: High-quality materials, easy installation, and improved performance.
* Cons: Can be pricey for some models, may require additional tuning.

### 2. **Sport Air Filter**

* **What is it?**: A high-flow air filter provides better airflow to the engine, allowing for improved performance and fuel efficiency.
* **Pros:** Increased power, better gas mileage, and a more aggressive sound.
* **Cons:** May require additional tuning, can be pricey for some models.

**Recommended Product:** **K&N High-Flow Air Filter** ($20-$50)

* Pros: High-quality materials, easy installation, and improved performance.
* Cons: May not fit all bike models, can be pricey for some models.

### 3. **Performance Exhaust**

* **What is it?**: A performance exhaust system provides a more aggressive sound and increased power by reducing backpressure.
* **Pros:** Increased power, better sound, and improved style.
* **Cons:** Can be pricey, may not fit all bike models, and may require additional tuning.

**Recommended Product:** **Borla Performance Exhaust** ($200-$500)

* Pros: High-quality materials, easy installation, and improved performance.
* Cons: Can be pricey, may not fit all bike models.
