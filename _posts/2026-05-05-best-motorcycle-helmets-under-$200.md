**Section 1: Safety Features to Look For**

Before we dive into our top picks, let's discuss the essential safety features to look for in a motorcycle helmet. A good helmet should have:

* A DOT (Department of Transportation) or Snell certification, which ensures that the helmet meets rigorous safety standards
* A sturdy shell that absorbs impact and protects the head from injury
* A comfortable, adjustable fit system to reduce fatigue and prevent movement while riding
* Good ventilation to prevent overheating and fogging
* A clear visor or shield to reduce glare and provide excellent visibility

When choosing the right helmet, consider the following tips:
